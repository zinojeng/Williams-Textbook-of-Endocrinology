## Key Points / 重點摘要

**EN:**
- Intrapituitary or parasellar masses are mostly benign pituitary adenomas.
- Nonadenomatous sellar masses are rare and are usually inflammatory processes, infiltrations, or metastatic deposits, or may arise from adjacent structures including aneurysms, meningiomas, or chordomas.
- Nonfunctioning pituitary adenomas are usually of gonadotroph or null cell origin and usually present with compressive features or are diagnosed as incidentalomas.
- Secreting pituitary adenomas exhibit unique syndromes due to excess production of prolactin (prolactinomas), growth hormone (acromegaly/gigantism), adrenocorticotrophin (Cushing disease), or very rarely, thyrotrophin or gonadotrophins.
- Management of pituitary masses includes surgery, radiation, and specific targeted medical therapies.

**中文：**
- 腦下垂體內或鞍旁腫塊絕大多數為良性腦下垂體腺瘤。
- 非腺瘤性鞍區腫塊少見，通常為發炎性病變、浸潤性疾病或轉移灶，也可能來自鄰近結構，包括動脈瘤、腦膜瘤（meningioma）或脊索瘤（chordoma）。
- 無功能性腦下垂體腺瘤通常源自促性腺激素細胞（gonadotroph）或裸細胞（null cell），多以壓迫症狀表現，或以偶發瘤（incidentaloma）形式被診斷。
- 分泌性腺瘤因激素過度分泌而呈現獨特症候群：泌乳素（prolactinoma）、生長激素（肢端肥大症／巨人症）、ACTH（庫欣氏病），或極罕見的 TSH 或促性腺激素。
- 腦下垂體腫塊的治療包括手術、放射治療與特異性標靶藥物治療。

## 1. Pituitary Mass Effects & Evaluation / 腦下垂體腫塊效應與評估

**EN:**
An expanding intrasellar mass compresses normal pituitary tissue and remodels the bony sella; the dorsal sellar roof offers least resistance. Pressure from below on the optic chiasm first affects superior temporal visual fields, extending to the entire temporal field and ultimately blindness with optic disc atrophy. Lateral extension into the cavernous sinus only rarely compromises cranial nerves III, IV, VI, and V1/V2. Headache is common even without suprasellar extension; intrasellar pressure was elevated two- to threefold in patients with pituitary failure, and stalk compression causes hyperprolactinemia by blocking dopamine access. Ninety-one percent of 1120 patients undergoing transsphenoidal surgery for sellar masses had pituitary adenomas; Rathke's cleft cysts account for up to 40% of nonadenomatous masses. Autopsy series show up to 20% of subjects harbor an incidental clinically silent adenoma, and microadenoma-compatible abnormalities appear in about 10% of normal adults on MRI.

**中文：**
擴張的鞍內腫塊會壓迫正常腦下垂體組織並重塑骨性蝶鞍；鞍膈上方阻力最小。由下方壓迫視交叉時，先影響<u>上顳側視野</u>，續而擴及整個顳側視野，最終可致失明併視神經盤萎縮。向外侧延伸侵入海綿竇時，第 III、IV、VI 對腦神經及第 V 對之眼支/上頜支僅罕見受損。即使無鞍上延伸，頭痛仍常見；合併腦下垂體功能低下者的鞍內壓力升高 2–3 倍，而垂體柄受壓因阻斷多巴胺輸送導致高泌乳素血症。1120 位因鞍區腫塊接受經蝶手術的患者中，91% 為腦下垂體腺瘤；Rathke 裂囊腫佔非腺瘤性腫塊達 40%。屍檢系列顯示高達 20% 的人帶有臨床沉默的偶發性腺瘤，約 10% 的正常成人 MRI 可見符合微腺瘤的異常。

## 2. Imaging & Neuroophthalmologic Assessment / 影像與神經眼科評估

**EN:**
Pituitary MRI (T1-weighted coronal and sagittal sections, slice thickness <3 mm, pre- and post-gadolinium) is the imaging of choice. During pregnancy the gland should not exceed 10 to 12 mm and the stalk should not exceed 4 mm; a thickened stalk suggests hypophysitis, granuloma, germinoma, or chordoma. Microadenomas usually appear hypodense after gadolinium, whereas macroadenomas are more vascular with higher gadolinium uptake. CT identifies bony invasion and calcifications characteristic of craniopharyngiomas and meningiomas. Currently fewer than 10% of patients present with visual symptoms; most pituitary-related defects are bitemporal visual loss, especially superior field portions, and defects marginate at the vertical midline. A 10-mm gap separates the optic chiasm and diaphragma sellae, so extensive suprasellar extension is required before vision is compromised.

**中文：**
腦下垂體 MRI（T1 加權冠狀與矢狀切面、切層厚度 <3 mm、gadolinium 顯影前後）為首選影像。懷孕期間腺體正常不應超過 10–12 mm，垂體柄不應超過 4 mm；<u>垂體柄增厚</u>提示垂體炎（hypophysitis）、肉芽腫、生殖細胞瘤（germinoma）或脊索瘤。微腺瘤在 gadolinium 注射後通常呈低訊號，而大腺瘤血管較豐富、顯影攝取較高。CT 可辨識骨質侵犯與顱咽管瘤、腦膜瘤特徵性的鈣化。目前僅不到 10% 患者以視覺症狀就診；腦下垂體相關視野缺損多為**雙顳側視野缺損**（尤以上方視野為著），且缺損以垂直中線為界。視交叉與鞍膈間有 10 mm 的間距，故需相當程度的鞍上延伸才會影響視力。

## 3. Management: Surgery, Radiation, Medical / 治療：手術、放射、藥物

**EN:**
Transsphenoidal surgery avoids the cranial cavity; most patients are ambulatory within 6 to 9 hours with a hospital stay of about 2 days. Surgery is primarily indicated for well-circumscribed GH-secreting adenomas, TSH-secreting adenomas, all ACTH-secreting adenomas, and often nonfunctioning macroadenomas. Among 234 patients assessed postoperatively, 45 of 93 with preoperative hypopituitarism recovered one to three axes. Transient AVP deficiency and SIADH may follow surgery; permanent complications occur in up to 10% and surgery-related mortality is up to 1%. Fractionated radiotherapy (45–50 Gy) halts growth in over 90% of nonsecreting adenomas by 10 years; within 10 years up to 80% of irradiated patients develop pituitary trophic hormone deficits (median time about 5 years after radiosurgery), and second brain tumors arose in 5% of irradiated patients versus 2.1% of controls at 20 years. Medical therapy targets dopamine D2 receptors and somatostatin receptors (SSTs) to suppress PRL, GH, and TSH hypersecretion and often shrink the mass.

**中文：**
經蝶手術（transsphenoidal surgery）不進入顱腔；多數患者 6–9 小時內可下床，住院約 2 天。手術主要適應症為界限清楚的 GH 分泌腺瘤、TSH 分泌腺瘤、<u>所有 ACTH 分泌腺瘤</u>，以及多數無功能性大腺瘤。234 位術後評估的患者中，93 位術前有腦下垂體功能低下者有 45 位恢復 1–3 條軸線功能。術後可出現暫時性 AVP 缺乏與 SIADH；永久性併發症可達 10%，手術相關死亡率最高 1%。分次放射治療（45–50 Gy）在 10 年內可遏止 >90% 無分泌性腺瘤的生長；照射後 10 年內高達 80% 患者出現腦下垂體激素缺乏（立體定位放射手術後中位發生時間約 5 年），20 年時續發腦瘤發生率為 5%（對照組 2.1%）。藥物治療以多巴胺 D2 受體與體抑素受體（SSTs）為標靶，抑制 PRL、GH、TSH 過度分泌並常使腫塊縮小。

## 4. Parasellar Masses / 鞍旁腫塊

**EN:**
Parasellar masses account for about 20% of all brain tumors. Craniopharyngiomas constitute about 3% of intracranial tumors and up to 10% of childhood brain tumors, with a bimodal age distribution (5–14 and 50–74 years); they arise from Rathke's pouch squamous remnants, contain cholesterol-rich viscous fluid and calcifications, and AVP deficiency is often the earliest feature. Adamantinomatous (CTNNB1) and papillary (BRAF V600E) variants harbor targetable mutations; postoperative recurrence occurs in about 20%, and hypothalamus-sparing surgery mitigates severe postoperative obesity (SMR 2.7). Meningiomas are isodense on T1 and T2 MRI; chordomas arise from notochord remnants with osteolytic erosion and a mean survival of about 5 years. Pituitary metastases (breast 37.2%, lung 24.2%) favor the posterior pituitary, commonly presenting with AVP deficiency. Parasellar aneurysms must be excluded before surgery.

**中文：**
鞍旁腫塊約佔所有腦腫瘤的 20%。顱咽管瘤（craniopharyngioma）約佔顱內腫瘤 3%、兒童腦瘤可達 10%，年齡呈雙峰分布（5–14 歲與 50–74 歲）；源自 Rathke 囊袋鱗狀上皮殘餘，內含富膽固醇黏稠囊液與鈣化，<u>AVP 缺乏（尿崩）常為最早表現</u>。Adamantinomatous 型（CTNNB1 突變）與乳突型（BRAF V600E 突變）具可標靶的突變；術後復發率約 20%，保留下視丘的手術可減輕術後嚴重肥胖（標準化死亡比 SMR 2.7）。腦膜瘤在 T1 與 T2 MRI 皆呈等訊號；脊索瘤源自脊索殘餘，伴溶骨性侵蝕，平均存活約 5 年。腦下垂體轉移瘤（乳癌 37.2%、肺癌 24.2%）好發於後葉（血源性供應直接來自內頸動脈），常以 AVP 缺乏表現。手術前必須排除鞍旁動脈瘤。

## 5. Hypophysitis / 垂體炎

**EN:**
Lymphocytic hypophysitis features lymphocytic and plasma cell infiltrates; more than half of patients present with headache, visual impairment, and hyperprolactinemia. Fifty-six percent have secondary hypoadrenalism, and 57% of pregnancy-associated cases occur in the last month of gestation or first 2 months postpartum. AVP deficiency occurs in up to 20%. High-dose glucocorticoids are a mainstay, though recurrences exceed 40% among responders. Other forms include granulomatous (sarcoidosis, tuberculosis), xanthomatous, necrotizing, and IgG4-related hypophysitis. Immune checkpoint inhibitor–induced hypophysitis occurs in 8% to 10% (up to 17%) of ipilimumab-treated patients (median onset 4 months); secondary adrenal insufficiency is the most common deficit (84%) and few patients recover endocrine function.

**中文：**
淋巴球性垂體炎（lymphocytic hypophysitis）以淋巴球與漿細胞浸潤為特徵；超過半數患者以頭痛、視力障礙及高泌乳素血症表現。56% 有續發性腎上腺功能低下；與懷孕相關的病例中 57% 發生於<u>妊娠最後一個月或產後前兩個月</u>。AVP 缺乏見於最多 20% 患者。高劑量糖皮質素為主要治療，但有效者復發率超過 40%。其他型態包括肉芽腫性（類肉瘤病、結核）、黃色瘤性、壞死性與 IgG4 相關垂體炎。免疫檢查點抑制劑誘發之垂體炎見於 8–10%（最高 17%）接受 ipilimumab 治療的患者（中位發病時間 4 個月）；最常見缺乏為**續發性腎上腺功能不全（84%）**，且少有患者恢復內分泌功能。

## 6. Apoplexy & Sheehan Syndrome / 垂體中風與 Sheehan 症候群

**EN:**
Pituitary apoplexy, an endocrine emergency, results from spontaneous hemorrhage into an adenoma or follows trauma, hypertension, diabetes, anticoagulation, or pregnancy. It evolves over 1 to 2 days with severe headache, ocular palsies, or visual field defects; acute adrenal insufficiency from ACTH loss is frequent. In 13 consecutive patients, baseline cortisol was <5 μg/dL in seven. Patients with visual compromise require urgent transsphenoidal surgery, and recovery correlates inversely with time elapsed since hemorrhage. Pituitary insufficiency becomes clinically apparent only when approximately 75% of the gland is damaged; 10% residual functional mass can mask complete failure. Sheehan syndrome after severe postpartum hemorrhage presents from hypovolemic shock with pituitary necrosis to gradual pituitary insufficiency; inability to nurse and postpartum amenorrhea are most prominent.

**中文：**
垂體中風（pituitary apoplexy）為內分泌急症，導因於腺瘤內自發性出血，或發生於頭部外傷、高血壓、糖尿病、抗凝血治療或懷孕之後。病程常於 1–2 天內演變，表現嚴重頭痛、眼肌麻痺或視野缺損；因 ACTH 喪失引起的<u>急性腎上腺功能不全</u>十分常見。13 位連續病例中 7 位基礎皮質醇 <5 μg/dL。有視覺受損者需緊急經蝶手術減壓，視力恢復與出血後經過時間呈負相關。腦下垂體約 75% 受損後功能不足才臨床顯現；10% 的殘餘功能細胞即可掩蓋完全性衰竭。Sheehan 症候群見於嚴重產後大出血之後，表現從低血容休克併垂體壞死到數月至數年間漸進性垂體功能不足；最突出的症狀為**無法哺乳與產後無月經**。

## 7. Epidemiology, Pathogenesis & Classification / 流行病學、致病機轉與分類

**EN:**
Pituitary adenomas account for about 15% of intracranial neoplasms, with population prevalence of approximately 77 to 115 per 100,000 (57% prolactinomas, 28% NFAs, 11% GH-secreting, 2% Cushing disease in the Banbury series). Adenomas arise from differentiated cell lineages defined by transcription factors: TPIT (corticotroph), PIT1 (somatotroph, lactotroph, thyrotroph), and SF1 (gonadotroph). Familial syndromes include MEN1, Carney complex, familial isolated pituitary adenomas (AIP), and X-LAG syndrome. Microadenomas are <10 mm, macroadenomas ≥10 mm; the Hardy and Knosp classifications grade sellar invasion and cavernous sinus involvement (Knosp 3–4 defines true invasion). Aggressive adenomas (~0.5%) show Knosp grade 3 or 4, growth >20% and ≥2 mm within 6 months, elevated Ki-67 (>3%), and increased mitotic activity; malignant adenomas with extracranial metastases represent 0.001% to 0.005%, most often secreting ACTH or PRL. Temozolomide yields initial responses in about half of aggressive/malignant cases.

**中文：**
腦下垂體腺瘤約佔顱內腫瘤 15%，族群盛行率約每 10 萬人 77–115 例（Banbury 系列中 prolactinoma 57%、NFA 28%、GH 分泌型 11%、庫欣氏病 2%）。腺瘤源自由轉錄因子界定的分化細胞譜系：TPIT（促腎上腺皮質細胞）、PIT1（促生長／泌乳／促甲狀腺細胞）、SF1（促性腺細胞）。家族性症候群包括 MEN1、Carney complex、家族性孤立性垂體腺瘤（AIP 突變）與 X-LAG 症候群。微腺瘤 <10 mm、大腺瘤 ≥10 mm；Hardy 與 Knosp 分級評估蝶鞍侵犯與海綿竇侵犯（<u>Knosp 3–4 級才定義為真正侵入海綿竇</u>）。侵襲性腺瘤（約 0.5%）具 Knosp 3–4 級、6 個月內生長 >20% 且 ≥2 mm、Ki-67 增殖指數 >3% 與有絲分裂活性升高；伴顱外轉移的惡性腺瘤僅佔 0.001–0.005%，最常分泌 ACTH 或 PRL。Temozolomide 對侵襲性/惡性病例約半數有初步療效。

## 8. Prolactin-Secreting Adenomas / 泌乳素分泌腺瘤

**EN:**
Prolactinomas account for more than 50% of pituitary neoplasms (annual incidence ~30 per 100,000). The female:male ratio for microadenomas is 20:1; men harbor larger (26 vs. 10 mm), more invasive adenomas with higher PRL. PRL >200 ng/mL strongly indicates a prolactinoma, and >500 ng/mL is invariably tumor-related; drug-induced or functional elevations usually do not exceed 150 ng/mL. Hyperprolactinemia suppresses GnRH pulsatility, causing amenorrhea, infertility, galactorrhea (up to 50% of women, 35% of men), erectile dysfunction, and bone loss. Beware the "hook effect" and macroprolactinemia (screen by PEG precipitation). Cabergoline (0.5–1 mg twice weekly) is first-line: PRL normalizes in 70% to 80%, and cabergoline shrinks the mass in most patients with better tolerance than bromocriptine. Transsphenoidal resection normalizes PRL in up to 90% of microprolactinomas (71% of 1224 pooled patients) and 50% to 75% of selected macroadenomas. During pregnancy, expansion risk is <5% for microprolactinomas and ~50% for macroprolactinomas; DAs are discontinued upon pregnancy confirmation, and cabergoline exposure has not increased malformation rates (3.4% vs. 6.4% in the general population).

**中文：**
Prolactinoma 佔腦下垂體腫瘤 50% 以上（年發生率約每 10 萬人 30 例）。微腺瘤女男比 20:1；男性腫瘤較大（26 vs. 10 mm）、較具侵襲性、PRL 較高。<u>PRL >200 ng/mL 強烈提示 prolactinoma，>500 ng/mL 幾乎必為腫瘤所致</u>；藥物性或功能性升高通常不超過 150 ng/mL。高泌乳素血症抑制 GnRH 脈衝分泌，導致無月經、不孕、乳漏（女性達 50%、男性 35%）、勃起功能障礙與骨質流失。須注意「hook effect」與巨泌乳素血症（macroprolactinemia，以 PEG 沉澱法篩檢）。Cabergoline（每週兩次 0.5–1 mg）為第一線：PRL 正常化率 70–80%，多數患者腫塊縮小，耐受性優於 bromocriptine。經蝶手術可使高達 90% 微腺瘤患者 PRL 正常化（1224 位彙整病例中為 71%），選擇性大腺瘤為 50–75%。懷孕期間微腺瘤擴大風險 <5%、大腺瘤約 50%；確認懷孕後停用多巴胺促效劑，cabergoline 暴露未增加畸形率（3.4% vs. 一般族群 6.4%）。

## 9. Nonfunctioning Pituitary Adenomas / 無功能性腦下垂體腺瘤

**EN:**
NFAs account for 30% to 55% of pituitary adenomas, mostly of gonadotroph origin (~64% immunostain for LH/FSH subunits). Most show some hypopituitarism: GH deficiency in up to 85% and hypogonadism in up to 75%; stalk-effect PRL elevation is usually ≤100 ng/mL. Under observation, macroadenomas enlarge in 50% to 60% versus 12.5% of microadenomas within 5 years (pooled incidentaloma data: 23% of macroadenomas vs. 10% of microadenomas enlarged). Transsphenoidal surgery is the primary treatment, improving visual defects in up to 80%; postoperative regrowth without radiotherapy occurs in roughly one-third, whereas SRS stabilizes or shrinks the mass in up to 90% at the cost of new hypopituitarism in 21% to 32%. Cabergoline stabilized growth in 87% of 79 treated patients. Silent corticotroph adenomas (up to 20% of resected adenomas, 2:1 male preponderance) are more aggressive, and serial MRI at 1, 2, and 5 years is advised.

**中文：**
NFA 佔腦下垂體腺瘤 30–55%，多源自促性腺細胞（約 64% 免疫染色呈 LH/FSH 次單位陽性）。多數患者有不同程度腦下垂體功能低下：GH 缺乏可達 85%、性腺功能低下可達 75%；垂體柄效應（stalk effect）造成的 PRL 升高通常 ≤100 ng/mL。觀察追蹤下，大腺瘤 5 年內增大者達 50–60%，微腺瘤僅 12.5%（偶發瘤彙整資料：大腺瘤 23% vs. 微腺瘤 10% 增大）。經蝶手術為主要治療，最高 80% 患者視野缺損改善；未接受放療者約三分之一術後再生長，立體定位放射手術（SRS）可穩定或縮小腫塊達 90%，但 21–32% 出現新發腦下垂體功能低下。Cabergoline 治療 79 位患者中 87% 腫瘤穩定。<u>沉默型促腎上腺皮質腺瘤（silent corticotroph adenoma）</u>佔切除腺瘤達 20%、男女比 2:1，行為較具侵襲性。建議於治療後第 1、2、5 年追蹤 MRI。

## 10. Functional Gonadotrophin-Secreting Adenomas / 功能性促性腺激素分泌腺瘤

**EN:**
A very small subset of gonadotroph adenomas secrete FSH, LH, and/or α-subunit; most are macroadenomas (~90% with suprasellar extension). High FSH with low LH is often the only clue. Premenopausal women present with menstrual disturbance, infertility, and ovarian hyperstimulation with multiseptated bilateral ovarian cysts; estradiol may be elevated up to 80-fold. In men, FSH may enlarge testes to 6 to 7 cm; LH-secreting adenomas may raise testosterone. DAs and SRLs are ineffective, and GnRH analogues may exacerbate hyperstimulation or provoke apoplexy; transsphenoidal resection is the definitive primary treatment, after which gonadotrophins normalize and ovarian cysts usually remit.

**中文：**

## 11. Acromegaly / 肢端肥大症

**EN:**
Acromegaly prevalence is 28 to 137 cases per million with annual incidence about 10 per million; more than 95% of patients harbor a GH-secreting pituitary adenoma (GSP mutations in ~30%), and macroadenomas predominate (>65%). Ectopic GHRH-secreting tumors and McCune-Albright syndrome are rare causes. Clinical features include acral enlargement, prognathism, hyperhidrosis, arthropathy, sleep apnea, cardiomyopathy, hypertension, and colon polyps; glucose intolerance/diabetes occurs in about 50% (diabetes hazard ratio 4.0). IGF1 is the screening test; diagnosis is confirmed by nadir GH <0.4 μg/L (ultrasensitive assay) during 75-g OGTT with elevated age-adjusted IGF1. Surgery normalizes IGF1 in 73% of microadenomas and 61% of macroadenomas; a 1-week postoperative OGTT nadir GH around 1 μg/L predicts 88.9% remission probability. SRLs (octreotide LAR, lanreotide) achieve GH control and IGF1 normalization in about 55% each; SST2 expression and dense granulation predict response, and pasireotide is superior (31% vs. 19% control) at the cost of hyperglycemia in about 57%. Pegvisomant, a GH-receptor antagonist, normalizes IGF1 in up to 90% in trials (63%–73% in surveillance); combination SRL plus weekly pegvisomant achieved 95% efficacy. SRS achieved endocrine remission in 59% (mean 38 months).

**中文：**
肢端肥大症盛行率為每百萬人 28–137 例，年發生率約每百萬人 10 例；>95% 患者帶有 GH 分泌腦下垂體腺瘤（約 30% 具 GSP 突變），且大腺瘤為主（>65%）。異位 GHRH 分泌腫瘤與 McCune-Albright 症候群為罕見病因。臨床特徵包括肢端肥大、下顎前突、多汗、關節病變、睡眠呼吸中止、心肌病變、高血壓與大腸息肉；葡萄糖耐受不良／糖尿病約見於 50%（糖尿病風險比 4.0）。IGF1 為篩檢首選；確診需 75 g 口服葡萄糖耐受試驗（OGTT）中 <u>GH 最低值 <0.4 μg/L（超敏感檢測法）且年齡校正 IGF1 升高</u>。手術可使 73% 微腺瘤與 61% 大腺瘤患者 IGF1 正常化；術後一週 OGTT GH 最低值約 1 μg/L 時，緩解機率為 88.9%。SRL（octreotide LAR、lanreotide）之 GH 控制率與 IGF1 正常化率各約 55%；SST2 表現與緻密顆粒型（densely granulated）預測療效佳，pasireotide 療效更優（31% vs. 19%）但約 57% 出現高血糖。GH 受體拮抗劑 pegvisomant 在試驗中可使高達 90% 患者 IGF1 正常化（真實世界監測 63–73%）；低劑量 SRL 併每週 pegvisomant 可達 95% 療效。SRS 內分泌緩解率 59%（平均 38 個月達成）。

## 12. ACTH-Secreting Adenomas (Cushing Disease) / ACTH 分泌腺瘤（庫欣氏病）

**EN:**
ACTH-secreting adenomas are the most frequent cause of endogenous Cushing syndrome, with an annual incidence of 1.6 cases per million; adenomas are typically small, often <2 mm. Screening comprises 24-hour UFC, late-night salivary cortisol, and the 1-mg overnight dexamethasone test (failure to suppress cortisol below 1.8 μg/dL). Standard 1.5T MRI detects only 50% of adenomas; inferior petrosal sinus sampling lateralizes ACTH secretion and distinguishes pituitary from ectopic sources. Transsphenoidal resection achieves remission (postoperative cortisol <2 ng/dL) in approximately 80% of microadenomas and 60% of macroadenomas; recurrence rates are 5% to 35%, sometimes 5 to 10 years later. Pasireotide normalizes cortisol in 20% to 40%; steroidogenesis inhibitors (ketoconazole, metyrapone, osilodrostat, mitotane, etomidate) and mifepristone are options, and Gamma Knife SRS achieves biochemical control in 80% at 5 years. Bilateral adrenalectomy requires monitoring for Nelson syndrome.

**中文：**
ACTH 分泌腺瘤是內源性庫欣氏症候群最常見的原因，年發生率每百萬人 1.6 例；腺瘤通常很小，常 <2 mm。篩檢包括 24 小時尿液游離皮質醇（UFC）、深夜唾液皮質醇及過夜 1 mg dexamethasone 抑制試驗（<u>皮質醇無法抑制至 <1.8 μg/dL</u>）。標準 1.5T MRI 僅能偵測 50% 的腺瘤；下岩靜脈竇採樣（IPSS）可側化 ACTH 分泌並區分腦下垂體與異位來源。經蝶手術緩解率（術後皮質醇 <2 ng/dL）微腺瘤約 80%、大腺瘤約 60%；復發率 5–35%，且可能遲至術後 5–10 年。Pasireotide 可使 20–40% 患者皮質醇正常化；類固醇合成抑制劑（ketoconazole、metyrapone、osilodrostat、mitotane、etomidate）與糖皮質素受體拮抗劑 mifepristone 為藥物選項，Gamma Knife SRS 於 5 年時生化控制率達 80%。雙側腎上腺切除後須監測 Nelson 症候群。

## 13. Thyrotrophin-Secreting Adenomas / TSH 分泌腺瘤

**EN:**
TSH-secreting adenomas are the rarest functional pituitary adenomas (0.5%–3%), usually diagnosed in the fifth or sixth decade. The hallmark is elevated T4 and T3 with inappropriately normal or elevated (nonsuppressed) TSH; 70% to 90% are macroadenomas, frequently invasive, and 30% co-secrete GH or PRL via the common PIT1 lineage. The differential diagnosis includes thyroid hormone resistance (no pituitary mass; an α-subunit/TSH molar ratio >1 favors adenoma in ~85%). Surgery is first-line, achieving biochemical control in 75% to 80%, but complete cures occur in fewer than 40% because of sinus invasion and fibrous consistency; about one-third require adjuvant radiotherapy. SRLs are the mainstay of medical therapy (octreotide suppressed TSH in 90% and shrank tumors in 50%). Antithyroid drugs or radioactive iodine should be avoided, as removing thyroid hormone feedback may promote invasive adenoma growth.

**中文：**
TSH 分泌腺瘤是最罕見的功能性腦下垂體腺瘤（0.5–3%），多在五、六十歲診斷。標誌性表現為 <u>T4、T3 升高伴不適當地正常或升高（未被抑制）的 TSH</u>；70–90% 為大腺瘤且常具侵襲性，30% 經共同 PIT1 譜系同時分泌 GH 或 PRL。鑑別診斷須考慮甲狀腺素阻抗症（無腦下垂體腫塊；α-次單位/TSH 莫耳比 >1 者約 85% 為腺瘤）。手術為第一線治療，生化控制率 75–80%，但因海綿竇/蝶竇侵犯與腫瘤質地纖維化，完全治癒率不到 40%；約三分之一需輔助放療。SRL 為藥物治療主力（octreotide 可抑制 90% 患者的 TSH、使 50% 腫瘤縮小）。**應避免抗甲狀腺藥物或放射性碘**，因去除甲狀腺素負回饋可能促進腺瘤侵襲性生長。
