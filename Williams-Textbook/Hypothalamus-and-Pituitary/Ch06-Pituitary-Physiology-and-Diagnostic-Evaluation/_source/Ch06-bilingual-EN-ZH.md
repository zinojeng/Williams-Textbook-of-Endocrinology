## Key Points / 重點摘要

**EN:**
- The pituitary gland orchestrates endocrine system integrity through central hypothalamic and peripheral hormonal signals.
- Pituitary cells are organized into structural and functional networks formed during embryonic development but modified throughout life.
- Differentiated lactotrophs, somatotrophs, gonadotrophs, corticotrophs, and thyrotrophs are each regulated by system-specific factors.
- The growth hormone axis controls growth in childhood while regulating energy and substrate metabolism throughout life.
- Prolactin, responsible primarily for milk production, is uniquely under tonic inhibitory hypothalamic control by dopamine.
- The hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis is the pivotal system subserving stress and survival, through effects on energy supply, fuel metabolism, immunity, and cardiovascular function.
- The hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis plays a pivotal role in puberty, reproduction, and fertility, controlling gametogenesis and sex steroid production.
- The hypothalamic-pituitary-thyroid axis is critical for development, growth, and cellular metabolism, mediated by thyroid hormones.
- Pituitary failure can arise from developmental, heritable, and acquired disorders and is diagnosed by baseline and provocative pituitary and target gland hormone testing.

- 腦下垂體整合下視丘中樞訊號與周邊激素訊號，維繫整個內分泌系統的完整性。
- 垂體細胞在胚胎期形成結構與功能網絡，並終生持續重塑。
- Lactotroph、somatotroph、gonadotroph、corticotroph、thyrotroph 五種分化細胞各受系統特異性因子調控。
- 生長激素軸在兒童期主控生長，並終生調節能量與受質代謝。
- 泌乳素主司乳汁製造，是唯一受下視丘多巴胺（dopamine）持續性抑制的垂體激素。
- 下視丘-垂體-腎上腺（HPA）軸是應對壓力與維繫生存的樞紐，作用涵蓋能量供應、燃料代謝、免疫與心血管功能。

## 1. Anatomy, Development, and Control of Hormone Secretion / 解剖、發育與激素分泌調控

**EN:**
The pituitary, in the bony sella turcica, comprises anterior, posterior, and vestigial intermediate lobes; the adult gland weighs ~600 mg, increasing up to 1 g in pregnancy. The cavernous sinuses contain cranial nerves III, IV, VI and the internal carotids; the optic chiasm above the diaphragma sella is vulnerable to expanding masses. Five anterior cell types secrete hormones: corticotrophs (POMC/ACTH), somatotrophs (GH), thyrotrophs (TSH), gonadotrophs (FSH/LH), lactotrophs (PRL). The hypothalamic portal circulation (from superior hypophyseal arteries via the median eminence) is the predominant anterior lobe supply, delivering hypothalamic releasing/inhibiting hormones without systemic dilution; inferior hypophyseal arteries mainly supply the posterior lobe. Stalk disruption compromises portal flow and often causes AVP deficiency. The anterior pituitary arises from Rathke's pouch (evident at 3 weeks); the portal system is established by 20 weeks; corticotrophs appear at 6 weeks, somatotrophs at 8, thyrotrophs/gonadotrophs by 12, but lactotrophs only after 24 weeks. Lineage determination follows a transcription factor cascade—Prop1 → Pit1 (POU1F1) for somatotroph/lactotroph/thyrotroph lineages, SF1/DAX1 for gonadotrophs, Tbx19 (Tpit) for corticotrophs—and mutations cause isolated or combined hormone deficiencies. Three tiers control secretion: hypothalamic hormones, peripheral negative feedback, and intrapituitary paracrine/autocrine factors, yielding pulsatile secretion of ACTH, GH, PRL, TSH, FSH, and LH.

**中文：**
腦下垂體位於蝶鞍（sella turcica）骨腔內，由前葉、後葉及退化的中間葉組成；成人垂體約重 600 mg，懷孕時可增至 1 g。兩側海綿竇內有第 III、IV、VI 對腦神經與內頸動脈；鞍膈上方的視交叉易受擴張腫塊壓迫。前葉五種分泌細胞：corticotroph（POMC/ACTH）、somatotroph（GH）、thyrotroph（TSH）、gonadotroph（FSH/LH）、lactotroph（PRL）。源自上垂體動脈、經正中隆起的下視丘門脈循環是前葉主要血供，可將下視丘釋放/抑制激素不經全身稀釋直接送達垂體；下垂體動脈主要供應後葉。垂體柄受損會阻斷門脈血流，常致 AVP 缺乏（尿崩症）。前葉源自 Rathke 囊（妊娠 3 週可辨識）；門脈系統於 20 週建立；corticotroph 於 6 週出現、somatotroph 8 週、thyrotroph/gonadotroph 12 週，lactotroph 則要 24 週後才分化完成。細胞譜系由轉錄因子級聯決定——Prop1 → Pit1（POU1F1）主導 somatotroph/lactotroph/thyrotroph 譜系、SF1/DAX1 決定 gonadotroph、Tbx19（Tpit）主導 corticotroph——這些因子突變造成單一或合併性激素缺乏。分泌受<u>三層調控</u>：下視丘激素、周邊負回饋、垂體內旁分泌/自分泌因子，形成 ACTH、GH、PRL、TSH、FSH、LH 的脈衝式分泌。

## 2. Prolactin / 泌乳素

**EN:**
Lactotrophs comprise 15–25% of anterior pituitary cells, most arising from GH-producing cells. PRL is a 199–amino acid, 23-kDa polypeptide (chromosome 6) circulating as monomeric "little" PRL (23 kDa, most bioactive), "big" PRL (48–56 kDa), and macroprolactin (>100 kDa, PRL–immunoglobulin complexes). The 16-kDa cleavage product (vasoinhibin) is implicated in peripartum cardiomyopathy. PRL is uniquely under tonic dopaminergic inhibition via D2 receptors; TRH, VIP, and estrogen stimulate it. Half-life is 26–47 minutes, with 4–14 secretory pulses/24h, highest during sleep; signaling is via JAK2–STAT5.

Hyperprolactinemia may be physiologic (pregnancy—up to 10-fold; suckling—~8.5-fold; stress; sleep), pathologic, or drug induced. PRL >2000 mU/L (~100 μg/L) is almost never due to stalk dysfunction alone, favoring prolactinoma. Up to 20% of hypothyroid patients and many with chronic renal failure have elevated PRL. Macroprolactinemia (screened by PEG precipitation; 22% of 2089 hyperprolactinemic samples) has little bioactivity or clinical consequence. Neuroleptics (dopamine receptor antagonists) are the classic drug cause—risperidone is a potent stimulator. Hallmarks are galactorrhea and reproductive dysfunction (oligo/amenorrhea and infertility in women, hypogonadism in men), since hyperprolactinemia suppresses LH/FSH. PRL deficiency—usually with other deficits (Sheehan syndrome; POU1F1/PROP1/LHX3/HESX1/IGSF1 mutations; anti-PIT1 or checkpoint-inhibitor hypophysitis)—manifests only as inability to lactate.

**中文：**
Lactotroph 占前葉細胞 15–25%，多由 GH 分泌細胞分化而來。PRL 為 199 個胺基酸、23 kDa 的多肽（第 6 號染色體），循環中有單體「little」PRL（23 kDa，活性最高）、「big」PRL（48–56 kDa）與巨泌乳素（macroprolactin，>100 kDa，PRL–免疫球蛋白複合物）。16-kDa 裂解產物（vasoinhibin）與周產期心肌病變相關。PRL 是唯一受多巴胺經 D2 受體持續抑制的垂體激素；TRH、VIP 與雌激素刺激其分泌。半衰期 26–47 分鐘，每 24 小時 4–14 個分泌脈衝，睡眠時最高；訊號傳遞經 JAK2–STAT5。

高泌乳素血症可為生理性（懷孕——達 10 倍；哺乳吸吮——約 8.5 倍；壓力；睡眠）、病理性或藥物引起。<u>PRL >2000 mU/L（約 100 μg/L）幾乎不會單純由垂體柄效應（stalk effect）造成</u>，強烈提示 prolactinoma。多達 20% 甲狀腺功能低下患者與許多慢性腎衰竭患者 PRL 升高。巨泌乳素血症（以 PEG 沉澱法篩檢；2089 份檢體中占 22%）生物活性低、臨床影響甚微。抗精神病藥（多巴胺受體拮抗劑）是典型藥物原因——risperidone 為強力刺激劑。主要表現為乳漏（galactorrhea）與生殖功能障礙（女性月經稀少/無月經、不孕；男性性腺低下），因高泌乳素血症抑制 LH/FSH。PRL 缺乏通常伴其他垂體缺陷（Sheehan 症候群；POU1F1/PROP1/LHX3/HESX1/IGSF1 突變；抗 PIT1 抗體或免疫檢查點抑制劑垂體炎），唯一已知表現為產後無法泌乳。

## 3. Growth Hormone / 生長激素

**EN:**
Somatotrophs comprise 35–45% of pituitary cells; the gland contains 5–15 mg GH. The hGH gene cluster (chromosome 17q22-24) contains five genes; GH-N is pituitary, GH-V/CS placental. GH is a 191–amino acid, 22-kDa protein (75% of secretion; a 20-kDa splice variant ~10%). GHRH and ghrelin (28–amino acid gastric peptide acting on the GHS receptor; requires GHRH) stimulate, somatostatin inhibits secretion. Secretion is episodic—two-thirds of daily output occurs at night with slow-wave sleep; it rises two- to threefold in puberty and declines exponentially from the third decade. GH signals through the GHR (class I cytokine receptor) via JAK2–STAT5; SOCS proteins terminate action. GH acts directly and via IGF-1: lipolytic, insulin-antagonistic, protein sparing, growth promoting; hepatic IGF-1 exerts tight negative feedback.

Adult GHD most commonly follows surgery/radiation for pituitary, parasellar, and brain tumors (~two-thirds of cases); mortality in hypopituitarism is about twice control (meta-analytic SMR 1.99, 95% CI 1.21–2.76). Up to 67% of children with idiopathic GHD test normal as adults, mandating retesting before adult GH continuation unless panhypopituitarism or a defined genetic cause exists. Diagnosis requires provocative testing: the insulin tolerance test (ITT) is the gold standard—normal peak GH >5 μg/L, severe GHD <3 μg/L; glucagon and macimorelin are validated alternatives, with thresholds reduced by obesity. Replacement starts at 0.15–0.3 mg/day titrated to age- and gender-normalized IGF-1; women on oral estrogen need higher doses. Fasting, poorly controlled type 1 diabetes, and oral estrogen enhance GH secretion with low IGF-1—contrasting with autonomous hypersecretion and elevated IGF-1 in acromegaly. GH is banned in sports (evidence supports only enhanced anaerobic sprint capacity) and is not recommended for anti-aging.

**中文：**
Somatotroph 占垂體細胞 35–45%；腺體含 5–15 mg GH。hGH 基因簇（17q22-24）含五個基因；GH-N 於垂體表現，GH-V/CS 於胎盤表現。GH 為 191 個胺基酸、22 kDa 蛋白（占分泌量 75%；20-kDa 剪接變異體約 10%）。GHRH 與 ghrelin（28 個胺基酸的胃源性胜肽，作用於 GHS 受體；需 GHRH 才能發揮作用）刺激分泌，體抑素（somatostatin）抑制之。分泌呈脈衝式——<u>每日總量三分之二於夜間隨慢波睡眠分泌</u>；青春期升至 2–3 倍，自第三個十年起呈指數下降。GH 經 GHR（第一類細胞激素受體）活化 JAK2–STAT5；SOCS 蛋白終止其作用。GH 直接作用並經 IGF-1 間接作用：促脂肪分解、拮抗胰島素、節省蛋白質、促進生長；肝臟 IGF-1 對垂體 GH 形成緊密負回饋。

成人 GHD 最常見於垂體、鞍旁及腦腫瘤之手術/放射治療後（約占三分之二）；垂體功能低下者死亡率約為對照兩倍（統合分析 SMR 1.99，95% CI 1.21–2.76）。多達 67% 兒童期「特發性 GHD」成年復測正常，故除非有全垂體功能低下或明確遺傳病因，<u>續用成人 GH 前必須復測</u>。診斷須激發試驗：ITT 為黃金標準——正常峰值 GH >5 μg/L，重度 GHD <3 μg/L；glucagon 與 macimorelin 為經驗證替代方案，切點須依肥胖下修。替代治療由 0.15–0.3 mg/day 起始，依年齡性別配對之 IGF-1 滴定；口服雌激素之女性需較高劑量。禁食、控制不佳的第 1 型糖尿病與口服雌激素會增強 GH 分泌但 IGF-1 偏低——與肢端肥大症之自主性高分泌合併 IGF-1 升高形成對比。GH 為運動禁藥（證據僅支持增強無氧衝刺能力），且不建議作為抗老化治療。

## 4. Adrenocorticotropic Hormone (ACTH) / 促腎上腺皮質素

**EN:**
Corticotrophs comprise ~20% of anterior pituitary cells and are the earliest fetal pituitary cell type. The POMC gene (chromosome 2p23) encodes a 266–amino acid pre-prohormone cleaved by prohormone convertase PC1 into ACTH(1-39) and β-lipotropin. ACTH (39 amino acids, 4.5 kDa) is the sole ligand of adrenal MC2R (requiring accessory protein MRAP); ACTH, β-LPH, and γ-LPH share the MC1R-activating heptapeptide causing hyperpigmentation in Addison disease. CRH (via CRH-R1/cAMP) and vasopressin stimulate, glucocorticoids inhibit POMC; cytokines (IL6, LIF) also activate the HPA axis. ACTH is secreted with circadian periodicity (rise from ~4 AM, peak before 7 AM, nadir 11 PM–3 AM; ~40 pulses/24h, amplitude-modulated); cortisol follows by 5–10 minutes, and the rhythm is lost in Cushing disease. Morning (8 AM) ACTH is 8–25 ng/L.

ACTH deficiency (congenital: TBX19/Tpit mutations; acquired: tumors, surgery, irradiation, hypophysitis) presents without hyperpigmentation, salt wasting, or hyperkalemia because mineralocorticoid secretion is preserved. Morning cortisol <3 μg/dL suggests deficiency; >18 μg/dL indicates normal reserve. Dynamic tests: ITT (glucose <40 mg/dL; normal cortisol >20 μg/dL), overnight metyrapone (normal ACTH >200 ng/L, 11-deoxycortisol >200 nmol/L), CRH stimulation (ACTH two- to fourfold at 30 min; cortisol >20 μg/dL), cosyntropin 250 μg (normal cortisol >500 nmol/L [20 μg/dL]); high- and 1-μg low-dose tests have similar accuracy (sensitivity 0.64–0.83, specificity 0.86–0.93). Replacement: hydrocortisone 15–20 mg/day in divided doses; mineralocorticoid is unnecessary in secondary hypoadrenalism. Once-daily dual-release hydrocortisone improved weight, metabolic, immune, and quality-of-life measures versus thrice-daily dosing. Ectopic ACTH: small cell lung cancers release intact POMC, whereas carcinoids process it to ACTH.

**中文：**
Corticotroph 占前葉細胞約 20%，是胎兒期最早出現的垂體細胞。POMC 基因（2p23）編碼 266 個胺基酸的前激素原，經 prohormone convertase PC1 裂解為 ACTH(1-39) 與 β-lipotropin。ACTH（39 個胺基酸、4.5 kDa）是腎上腺 MC2R 的唯一配體（需輔助蛋白 MRAP）；ACTH、β-LPH、γ-LPH 共有可活化 MC1R 的七肽核心，是 Addison 病色素沉著的原因。CRH（經 CRH-R1/cAMP）與血管加壓素刺激、糖皮質素抑制 POMC；細胞激素（IL6、LIF）亦活化 HPA 軸。ACTH 呈日夜節律分泌（約凌晨 4 點上升、7 點前達峰、23 點至凌晨 3 點最低；每 24 小時約 40 個脈衝，以振幅調節）；皮質醇落後 ACTH 5–10 分鐘，此節律於庫欣氏病喪失。清晨（8 點）ACTH 為 8–25 ng/L。

ACTH 缺乏（先天性：TBX19/Tpit 突變；後天性：腫瘤、手術、放射、垂體炎）<u>不會出現色素沉著、失鹽或高血鉀</u>，因鹽皮質素分泌保留。清晨皮質醇 <3 μg/dL 提示缺乏；>18 μg/dL 表示儲備正常。動態試驗：ITT（血糖 <40 mg/dL；正常皮質醇 >20 μg/dL）、午夜 metyrapone（正常 ACTH >200 ng/L、11-deoxycortisol >200 nmol/L）、CRH 刺激（30 分鐘 ACTH 升至 2–4 倍；皮質醇 >20 μg/dL）、cosyntropin 250 μg（正常皮質醇 >500 nmol/L [20 μg/dL]）；高劑量與 1-μg 低劑量試驗準確度相近（敏感度 0.64–0.83、特異度 0.86–0.93）。替代治療：hydrocortisone 每日 15–20 mg 分次；次發性腎上腺低下無須補充鹽皮質素。每日一次雙釋放型 hydrocortisone 相較一日三次給藥，可改善體重、代謝、免疫與生活品質。異位 ACTH：小細胞肺癌傾向釋放完整 POMC，類癌（carcinoid）則加工產生 ACTH。

## 5. Gonadotropins (LH/FSH) / 促性腺激素

**EN:**
Gonadotrophs comprise 10–15% of anterior pituitary cells. LH and FSH are heterodimeric glycoproteins sharing a common α-subunit with TSH and hCG (αGSU, LHβ, FSHβ genes on chromosomes 6, 11, 19). Regulation integrates hypothalamic GnRH, intrapituitary activin/follistatin, and gonadal steroid/inhibin feedback. Kisspeptin (KISS1/KISS1R) is the key GnRH secretagogue; neurokinin B modulates kisspeptin, and MKRN3 restrains it prepubertally (MKRN3/DLK1 defects cause central precocious puberty); leptin links energy status to reproduction. A postnatal gonadotropin surge peaking at 2–3 months provides a window to assess HPG integrity. FSH drives follicular growth, granulosa aromatization of theca-derived androstenedione to estradiol, and inhibin production; the midcycle LH surge triggers ovulation and luteinization. In males, LH drives Leydig-cell testosterone synthesis; FSH acts on Sertoli cells for spermatogenesis.

GnRH stimulation (25–100 μg) raises LH within 20–30 minutes (peak 8–34 mIU/mL) but cannot reliably separate hypothalamic from pituitary lesions; it is used mainly for pubertal assessment, with GnRH-agonist (leuprolide) and kisspeptin tests as alternatives. Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (HH) may be congenital—Kallmann syndrome (KAL1/ANOS1, FGFR1, PROK2/PROKR2, CHD7) combines GnRH deficiency with anosmia, whereas GnRH secretion/action defects (KISS1R, TAC3/TACR3, GNRH1) are normosmic—or acquired (functional: stress, malnutrition, excessive exercise, low body weight; drugs: opiates, glucocorticoids; organic: tumors, infiltration, surgery, radiotherapy). Congenital HH males show small testes (<4 mL), micropenis, cryptorchidism, eunuchoid habitus; females show primary amenorrhea. Gonadotropins are inappropriately low with low sex steroids. Management: sex steroid replacement; gonadotropin or pulsatile GnRH therapy when fertility is desired.

**中文：**
Gonadotroph 占前葉細胞 10–15%。LH 與 FSH 為異二聚體醣蛋白，與 TSH、hCG 共用 α 次單元（αGSU、LHβ、FSHβ 基因分別位於第 6、11、19 號染色體）。調控整合下視丘 GnRH、垂體內 activin/follistatin，與性腺類固醇/inhibin 回饋。Kisspeptin（KISS1/KISS1R）是最關鍵的 GnRH 促泌因子；neurokinin B 調控 kisspeptin，MKRN3 於青春期前抑制之（MKRN3/DLK1 缺陷造成中樞性性早熟）；leptin 將能量狀態與生殖連結。出生後促性腺激素於 2–3 個月大達峰（「迷你青春期」），為評估 HPG 軸完整性的時間窗。FSH 驅動濾泡生長、顆粒細胞將莢膜細胞來源 androstenedione 芳香化為雌二醇並產生 inhibin；月經中期 LH 高峰觸發排卵與黃體化。男性中 LH 驅動 Leydig 細胞睪固酮合成；FSH 作用於 Sertoli 細胞支持精子生成。

GnRH 刺激試驗（25–100 μg）於 20–30 分鐘內升高 LH（峰值 8–34 mIU/mL），但無法可靠區分下視丘與垂體病灶；主要用於青春期評估，替代方案為 GnRH 促效劑（leuprolide）與 kisspeptin 試驗。低促性腺素性性腺低下（HH）可為先天性——Kallmann 症候群（KAL1/ANOS1、FGFR1、PROK2/PROKR2、CHD7）合併 GnRH 缺乏與嗅覺喪失，而 GnRH 分泌/作用缺陷（KISS1R、TAC3/TACR3、GNRH1）嗅覺正常——或後天性（功能性：壓力、營養不良、過度運動、低體重；藥物：鴉片類、糖皮質素；器質性：腫瘤、浸潤、手術、放射）。先天性 HH 男性表現小睪丸（<4 mL）、小陰莖、隱睪、類無睪體型；女性為原發性無月經。促性腺激素在性類固醇低下時呈不當偏低。治療：性類固醇替代；欲生育者採促性腺激素或脈衝式 GnRH。

## 6. Thyroid-Stimulating Hormone (TSH) / 促甲狀腺素

**EN:**
Thyrotrophs comprise ~5% of anterior pituitary cells; differentiation requires GATA2, POU1F1, and PROP1. TSH is a 28-kDa heterodimer (α-subunit gene on chromosome 6, TSHβ on chromosome 1). TRH stimulates; thyroid hormone (via TRβ2), somatostatin, and dopamine suppress TSH. Hypothalamic tanycytes expressing deiodinase 2 convert T4 to T3 for central negative feedback; deiodinase 3 inactivates thyroid hormones, and transporters (MCT8, OATP1, LAT) mediate cell entry—MCT8 mutations cause severe neurologic disease with high T3 and low T4. Daily TSH production is 100–400 mU (half-life ~50 min), enhanced up to 15-fold in hypothyroidism; circadian TSH peaks between 11 PM and 5 AM, not sleep entrained.

Third-generation assays (detection 0.01–0.02 mU/L) discriminate thyroid states, but TSH alone cannot diagnose central hypothyroidism—basal TSH is subnormal in only ~one-third of secondary hypothyroidism; the typical profile is low T4 with low/normal/minimally elevated TSH. The TRH test (200–500 μg IV) distinguishes TSH-secreting adenoma (no TSH rise) from thyroid hormone resistance (TSH rises); both show high TSH with high thyroid hormones. TSH deficiency arises from TSHβ/TRH-receptor mutations or developmental defects (LHX3, PROP1, POU1F1). Treatment: L-thyroxine 50–200 μg/day titrated to mid-normal free T4—TSH cannot guide dosing, and adrenal status must be evaluated and treated before starting thyroxine to avoid precipitating adrenal crisis.

**中文：**
Thyrotroph 占前葉細胞約 5%；分化依賴 GATA2、POU1F1 與 PROP1。TSH 為 28 kDa 異二聚體（α 次單元基因於第 6 號、TSHβ 於第 1 號染色體）。TRH 刺激 TSH；甲狀腺激素（經 TRβ2）、體抑素與多巴胺抑制之。下視丘伸長細胞（tanycyte）表現第 2 型去碘酶，將 T4 轉為 T3 執行中樞負回饋；第 3 型去碘酶去活化甲狀腺激素，轉運蛋白（MCT8、OATP1、LAT）介導激素入胞——MCT8 突變造成嚴重神經異常，伴 T3 高、T4 低。TSH 每日產量 100–400 mU（半衰期約 50 分鐘），甲低時可增至 15 倍；日夜節律於 23 點至凌晨 5 點達峰，與睡眠無關。

第三代檢測（偵測極限 0.01–0.02 mU/L）可區分甲狀腺功能狀態，但<u>單靠 TSH 無法診斷中樞性甲低</u>——次發性甲低僅約三分之一患者基礎 TSH 偏低；典型樣態為 T4 低、TSH 低/正常/輕微升高。TRH 試驗（200–500 μg IV）可鑑別 TSH 分泌腺瘤（TSH 不升）與甲狀腺激素抗性（TSH 上升）；兩者皆呈 TSH 與甲狀腺激素同時升高。TSH 缺乏源自 TSHβ/TRH 受體突變或發育基因缺陷（LHX3、PROP1、POU1F1）。治療：L-thyroxine 50–200 μg/day，滴定至游離 T4 位於正常範圍中段——不能以 TSH 指導劑量，且<u>開始補充甲狀腺素前必須先評估並處理腎上腺功能</u>，以免誘發腎上腺危機。

## 7. Developmental, Genetic, and Acquired Causes of Pituitary Failure / 垂體功能衰竭的病因

**EN:**
Hypopituitarism has an estimated prevalence of 46 per 100,000, with excess mortality that is primarily vascular. Developmental causes include midline anomalies and pituitary stalk interruption syndrome (PSIS: thin/interrupted stalk, anterior pituitary hypoplasia, ectopic posterior pituitary). Heritable causes: PROP1 mutations are the most common genetic cause of combined pituitary hormone deficiency (GH, PRL, TSH, LH/FSH, later-onset ACTH deficiency); POU1F1 causes GH/PRL/TSH deficiency; HESX1 associates with septo-optic dysplasia; TBX19 causes neonatal isolated ACTH deficiency; SOX2/SOX3/OTX2, IGSF1, NR5A1, NR0B1, and NFKB2 (DAVID syndrome) each yield characteristic deficits. Acquired causes: after traumatic brain injury, 15% have a single and 4% multiple deficits (GH 13% > ACTH 12% > LH/FSH 10% > TSH 4%); GHD prevalence reaches 45% in retired boxers. Subarachnoid hemorrhage causes pituitary dysfunction in ~46% acutely and 25% at 6 months. Radiation effects are dose and time dependent—GH is the most radiosensitive axis (deficiency from 18 Gy; 50–100% at 30–50 Gy within 3–5 years), TSH the most resistant. Immune checkpoint inhibitor hypophysitis occurs in 3–4% with anti-CTLA4 (6–8% with combined anti-PD1), typically 8–10 weeks after starting; of those affected, 83% have hypoadrenalism, 77% hypothyroidism, 53% hypogonadism.

The order of hormone loss with pituitary compression is usually GH > FSH > LH > TSH > ACTH; the corticotroph is most resistant and PRL deficiency exceedingly rare. All patients with hypothalamic-pituitary masses should be screened with baseline and provocative testing. About one-third recover function after surgical decompression, whereas ~25% lose further function postoperatively—hence annual screening. Replacement therapy spans hydrocortisone, L-thyroxine, sex steroids (gonadotropins or GnRH for fertility), somatotropin, and desmopressin.

**中文：**
垂體功能低下盛行率估計為每 10 萬人 46 例，死亡率過高且主因為血管性疾病。發育性病因包括中線異常與垂體柄中斷症候群（PSIS：垂體柄纖細/中斷、前葉發育不全、異位後葉）。遺傳性病因：<u>PROP1 突變是合併性垂體激素缺乏（CPHD）最常見的遺傳原因</u>（GH、PRL、TSH、LH/FSH 缺乏，ACTH 缺乏常較晚出現）；POU1F1 造成 GH/PRL/TSH 缺乏；HESX1 與視隔發育不良相關；TBX19 造成新生兒單獨性 ACTH 缺乏；SOX2/SOX3/OTX2、IGSF1、NR5A1、NR0B1 與 NFKB2（DAVID 症候群）各有特徵性缺陷組合。後天性病因：創傷性腦損傷（TBI）後 15% 有單一軸、4% 有多軸缺乏（GH 13% > ACTH 12% > LH/FSH 10% > TSH 4%）；退役拳擊手 GHD 盛行率達 45%。蛛網膜下腔出血急性期約 46% 有垂體功能異常，6 個月後為 25%。放射影響與劑量及時間相關——<u>GH 軸最具放射敏感性</u>（18 Gy 即可致缺乏；30–50 Gy 於 3–5 年內達 50–100%），TSH 軸最具抗性。免疫檢查點抑制劑垂體炎於抗 CTLA4 治療發生率 3–4%（合併抗 PD1 為 6–8%），多在開始治療後 8–10 週出現；受影響者 83% 腎上腺功能低下、77% 甲狀腺功能低下、53% 性腺功能低下。

垂體受壓時激素喪失順序通常為 <u>GH > FSH > LH > TSH > ACTH</u>；corticotroph 最具抗性、最晚喪失功能，PRL 缺乏極為罕見。所有下視丘-垂體腫塊患者皆應以基礎值與激發試驗篩檢垂體功能。約三分之一患者於手術減壓後功能恢復，但約 25% 術後功能進一步喪失——故應每年追蹤篩檢。替代治療涵蓋 hydrocortisone、L-thyroxine、性類固醇（欲生育者用促性腺激素或 GnRH）、somatotropin 與 desmopressin。
