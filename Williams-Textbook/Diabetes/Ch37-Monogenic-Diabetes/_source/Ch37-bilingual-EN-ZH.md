## Key Points / 重點摘要

**EN:**
- Monogenic forms of diabetes account for 1% to 2% of diabetes cases, resulting from gene defects that interfere with β-cell development and function (insulin production and secretion) or insulin action.
- Monogenic diabetes includes neonatal diabetes (onset generally under 9 months of age) and maturity-onset diabetes of the young (MODY), with childhood or young adult–onset, typically diagnosed before 30 years of age.
- The most common causes of neonatal diabetes are pathogenic variants in the insulin gene (*INS*, requiring insulin treatment) and in *KCNJ11* and *ABCC8*, which encode the subunits of the K-ATP channel and are usually responsive to high doses of sulfonylureas.
- The four most common causes of MODY: *GCK* (mild fasting hyperglycemia, little to no diabetes complications, generally no glucose-lowering treatment needed); *HNF1A* (glycosuria with progressive hyperglycemia, frequently responsive to low-dose sulfonylureas); *HNF4A* (transient neonatal hypoglycemia then progressive hyperglycemia, also sulfonylurea-responsive); *HNF1B* (renal cysts, pancreatic hypoplasia and exocrine dysfunction, hypomagnesemia; lacks clear precision therapy).
- Monogenic causes of lipodystrophies, insulin resistance, and obesity may also include diabetes as part of the syndrome.
- Syndromic forms account for <1% of children with diabetes, e.g., maternally inherited diabetes and deafness (MIDD, mitochondrial variants) and Wolfram syndrome (*WFS1* pathogenic variants).

**中文：**
- 單基因糖尿病佔所有糖尿病病例的 1–2%，源自干擾 β 細胞發育與功能（胰島素製造與分泌）或胰島素作用的單一基因缺陷。
- 單基因糖尿病包括新生兒糖尿病（發病通常在 9 個月大以前）與年輕人成年型糖尿病（MODY，兒童或年輕成人發病，通常在 30 歲前確診）。
- 新生兒糖尿病最常見的原因為胰島素基因（*INS*，需胰島素治療）以及編碼 K-ATP 通道次單元的 *KCNJ11* 與 *ABCC8* 基因的致病變異，後兩者通常對高劑量磺醯脲類（sulfonylurea）有反應。
- MODY 四大最常見原因：*GCK*（輕度空腹高血糖，糖尿病相關併發症極少或無，一般不需降血糖治療）；*HNF1A*（糖尿伴進行性高血糖，常對低劑量磺醯脲類有效）；*HNF4A*（新生兒期短暫低血糖史，之後進行性高血糖，同樣常對低劑量磺醯脲類有效）；*HNF1B*（腎囊腫、胰臟發育不全與外分泌功能障礙、低血鎂，尚無明確精準治療）。
- 脂肪失養症（lipodystrophy）、胰島素阻抗與肥胖的單基因病因，也可能以糖尿病作為症候群的一部分表現。
- 症候群型糖尿病佔兒童糖尿病不到 1%，例如粒線體致病變異造成的母系遺傳糖尿病合併耳聾（MIDD），以及 *WFS1* 致病變異造成的 Wolfram 症候群等。

## 1. Introduction & History / 前言與歷史

**EN:**
Monogenic diabetes is defined as a heterogeneous group of monogenic diseases including both neonatal diabetes and MODY. MODY specifically refers to a family of autosomal-dominant monogenic diabetes including GCK-MODY, HNF1A-MODY, HNF4A-MODY, and HNF1B-MODY. Because more than 30 associated genes have now been identified, the "MODY-X" nomenclature is avoided in favor of gene-based naming (e.g., HNF1A-MODY rather than MODY 3). Starting in 1958, Stefan Fajans studied the Michigan "R-W pedigree," now spanning seven generations, in which 74 members had non–insulin-dependent diabetes with unusually early onset. Robert Tattersall described three additional families with dominantly inherited diabetes effectively treated with sulfonylureas for over 40 years; in 1974 Fajans and Tattersall named this autosomal-dominant form MODY.

**中文：**
單基因糖尿病定義為包含新生兒糖尿病與 MODY 的一組異質性單基因疾病。MODY 特指體染色體顯性遺傳的單基因糖尿病家族，包括 GCK-MODY、HNF1A-MODY、HNF4A-MODY 與 HNF1B-MODY。由於目前已鑑定出超過 30 個相關基因，本章避免使用「MODY-X」編號命名法，改採以基因名稱為主的命名（例如以 HNF1A-MODY 取代 MODY 3）。自 1958 年起，Stefan Fajans 開始研究密西根州的「R-W 家系」（現已跨越七個世代），其中 74 名成員患有發病異常提早的非胰島素依賴型糖尿病。Robert Tattersall 另描述三個顯性遺傳糖尿病家族，以磺醯脲類有效治療超過 40 年；1974 年 Fajans 與 Tattersall 將此體染色體顯性遺傳型式命名為 MODY。

## 2. Precision Medicine / 精準醫療

**EN:**
The ADA/EASD Precision Medicine in Diabetes Initiative (PMDI) recognizes that "clinical implementation is already a reality for precision medicine in monogenic forms of diabetes." **Precision diagnosis** uses a three-stage biomarker screening pathway: (1) assessment of endogenous insulin secretion by serum C-peptide or urinary C-peptide/creatinine ratio (UCPCR); (2) if serum C-peptide ≥0.60 ng/mL (0.2 nmol/L) or UCPCR ≥0.2 nmol/mmol, measurement of GAD and IA-2 islet autoantibodies; (3) if both autoantibodies are negative, molecular genetic testing. **Precision therapeutics** targeting the molecular etiology result in stable or improved diabetes control and are cost-effective or cost-saving. **Precision prognosis and monitoring** exemplars include improved neurocognitive outcomes with early sulfonylurea treatment in K-ATP–related neonatal diabetes, and recognition of increased cardiovascular risk warranting statin therapy in HNF1A-MODY. The main hindrances to implementation are timely recognition, accurate genetic diagnosis, and prompt therapeutic action.

**中文：**
ADA/EASD 的糖尿病精準醫療倡議（PMDI）指出：「精準醫療在單基因糖尿病的臨床落地已是現實。」**精準診斷**採三階段生物標記篩檢路徑：(1) 以血清 C-peptide 或尿液 C-peptide/肌酸酐比值（UCPCR）評估內生性胰島素分泌；(2) 若血清 C-peptide ≥0.60 ng/mL（0.2 nmol/L）或 UCPCR ≥0.2 nmol/mmol，檢測 GAD 與 IA-2 胰島自體抗體；(3) 若兩種自體抗體皆陰性，進行分子基因檢測。**精準治療**針對分子病因，可使血糖控制穩定或改善，且具成本效益甚至可節省成本。**精準預後與監測**的兩個典範：K-ATP 通道相關新生兒糖尿病早期改用磺醯脲類可改善神經認知結局；HNF1A-MODY 心血管風險升高，需使用 statin 治療。落實精準醫療的主要障礙為及時辨識、正確的基因診斷，以及診斷後迅速採取治療行動。

## 3. Epidemiology / 流行病學

**EN:**
Monogenic diabetes is estimated at approximately 1% to 4% of all patients diagnosed with diabetes before age 30 years. UK genetic testing (2072 probands, 1280 relatives, 1996–2009) yielded a minimum MODY prevalence of 108 cases per million (~1% of diabetes cases), varying from 5.3 per million in Northern Ireland to 28.4 in Scotland, and from 8.2 in London to 48.9 in South West England. In the US SEARCH study, 8.2% (47/586) of sequenced pediatric participants carried a pathogenic variant in *GCK*, *HNF1A*, or *HNF4A* (1.2% of the original cohort); only 3 of 47 had a clinical MODY diagnosis, and most were treated with insulin. In the University of Chicago Monogenic Diabetes Registry (>3800 enrolled as of 2021; 1120 [43%] with a known genetic cause), *GCK* variants were the most common MODY cause (59%), followed by *HNF1A* (28%); *KCNJ11* was the most common neonatal diabetes gene (35%), followed by *INS* (16%). *HNF1A* penetrance is approximately 63% by age 25, 93.6% by age 50, and 98.7% by age 75. Permanent neonatal diabetes prevalence in US youth is estimated at 1 in 252,000.

**中文：**
單基因糖尿病估計約佔 30 歲前確診糖尿病患者的 1–4%。英國基因檢測資料（1996–2009 年間 2072 名先證者與 1280 名親屬）推算 MODY 最低盛行率為每百萬 108 例（約佔糖尿病病例 1%），地區差異大：北愛爾蘭每百萬 5.3 例、蘇格蘭 28.4 例；英格蘭各區從倫敦 8.2 例到西南部 48.9 例。美國 SEARCH 研究中，接受定序的兒科受試者有 8.2%（47/586）帶有 *GCK*、*HNF1A* 或 *HNF4A* 致病變異（佔原始世代 1.2%）；47 人中僅 3 人曾獲臨床 MODY 診斷，多數以胰島素治療，凸顯精準診斷的價值。芝加哥大學單基因糖尿病登錄（至 2021 年超過 3800 人登錄，其中 1120 人 [43%] 有已知基因病因）中，*GCK* 變異為最常見 MODY 病因（59%），其次為 *HNF1A*（28%）；新生兒糖尿病基因以 *KCNJ11* 最常見（35%），其次為 *INS*（16%）。*HNF1A* 外顯率 25 歲約 63%、50 歲 93.6%、75 歲 98.7%。美國青少年永久性新生兒糖尿病盛行率估計為 25.2 萬分之一。

## 4. Clinical Characteristics / 臨床特徵

**EN:**
At diagnosis, MODY cannot reliably be distinguished from type 1 and type 2 diabetes on clinical grounds alone. Features atypical for type 1 diabetes include absence of islet autoantibodies, measurable C-peptide (≥0.60 ng/mL [≥0.2 nmol/L]) with hyperglycemia, low insulin requirement, and lack of ketoacidosis when insulin is omitted; features atypical for type 2 diabetes include onset before 45 years, lack of significant obesity, lack of acanthosis nigricans, and normal triglycerides with normal/elevated HDL. Early onset, insulin independence, and autosomal-dominant inheritance suggest MODY, though *HNF1B* deletions are 50–60% de novo. Obesity does not exclude MODY: in the TODAY study of overweight/obese, antibody-negative, C-peptide–positive adolescents diagnosed with type 2 diabetes, 4.5% carried pathogenic or likely pathogenic MODY variants. The MODY Calculator (diabetesgenes.org), developed in a White European population and applicable to patients diagnosed under 35 years, estimates MODY probability from clinical data to guide genetic testing; type 1 diabetes genetic risk scores (T1D-GRS) also aid discrimination but perform less well outside European-ancestry populations.

**中文：**
在確診時，僅憑臨床特徵無法可靠區分 MODY 與第 1、2 型糖尿病。不像第 1 型糖尿病的特徵包括：胰島自體抗體陰性、高血糖時仍可測得 C-peptide（≥0.60 ng/mL [≥0.2 nmol/L]）、胰島素需求量低、停用胰島素也不出現酮酸中毒；不像第 2 型糖尿病的特徵包括：45 歲前發病、無明顯肥胖、無黑色棘皮症、三酸甘油酯正常且 HDL 正常或偏高。早發、不依賴胰島素、體染色體顯性遺傳皆提示 MODY，但 *HNF1B* 缺失有 50–60% 為新生（de novo）突變。肥胖不能排除 MODY：TODAY 研究中被診斷為第 2 型糖尿病、抗體陰性且 C-peptide 陽性的過重/肥胖青少年，有 4.5% 帶有致病或可能致病的 MODY 變異。MODY 計算器（diabetesgenes.org；以白人歐洲族群開發，僅適用於 35 歲前確診者）可依臨床資料估算 MODY 機率以引導基因檢測；第 1 型糖尿病基因風險分數（T1D-GRS）亦有助鑑別，但在非歐洲血統族群表現較差。

## 5. GCK-MODY（原 MODY 2）

**EN:**
GCK-MODY results from loss-of-function pathogenic variants in the glucokinase gene (*GCK*, chromosome 7), decreasing β-cell glucose sensing. Patients have stable, mild fasting hyperglycemia (generally 99–144 mg/dL [5.5–8.0 mmol/L]) present from birth, usually discovered incidentally; islet antibodies are negative, and OGTT typically shows an increment of less than 90 mg/dL (5.0 mmol/L) between fasting and 2-hour values. The phenotype is uniform despite variant severity. Patients are usually misclassified as type 1 or type 2 diabetes, but there is a lack of glycemic response to oral agents or insulin; treatment is not recommended — even contraindicated — because long-term microvascular and macrovascular complications are largely absent. Nonproliferative retinopathy is the only microvascular complication reported after an average 50-year exposure to mild hyperglycemia. Pregnancy is the exception in which treatment may be recommended. Rare co-occurrence with type 1 or type 2 diabetes should be suspected when glycemia worsens beyond the expected range.

**中文：**
GCK-MODY 源自第 7 號染色體葡萄糖激酶基因（*GCK*）的功能喪失型致病變異，使 β 細胞的葡萄糖感知能力下降。患者自出生即有穩定的輕度空腹高血糖（通常 99–144 mg/dL [5.5–8.0 mmol/L]），多為偶然發現；胰島抗體陰性，OGTT 通常顯示空腹與 2 小時血糖增幅小於 90 mg/dL（5.0 mmol/L）。無論變異嚴重度為何，表型相當一致。患者常被誤判為第 1 或第 2 型糖尿病，但對口服降血糖藥或胰島素缺乏血糖反應；由於長期微血管與大血管併發症幾乎不存在，不建議（甚至禁忌）給予降血糖治療。在平均暴露於輕度高血糖 50 年後，非增殖性視網膜病變是唯一被報告的微血管併發症。妊娠是唯一可能建議治療的例外。若血糖惡化超出 GCK-MODY 預期範圍，應懷疑罕見的合併第 1 型或第 2 型糖尿病。

## 6. HNF1A-MODY（原 MODY 3）

**EN:**
HNF1A-MODY arises from heterozygous-dominant variants in the transcription factor HNF1A, expressed mainly in liver, pancreatic β-cells, and kidney; point pathogenic variants cluster in the DNA-binding domain. Early presentation features mild fasting hyperglycemia and glucosuria at a low renal threshold — because HNF1A is a key factor for SGLT2 expression, glycosuria before diabetes onset is a hallmark of HNF1A insufficiency. The insulin secretory defect is progressive; complications can be as severe as unmanaged type 2 diabetes if untreated. Other features include hepatocellular adenomas, hyperlipidemia, and increased cardiovascular mortality. Most patients respond well to low-dose sulfonylureas (even to the point of hypoglycemia), though progressive failure may later require insulin; meglitinides achieve lower postprandial glucose with less delayed hypoglycemia. SGLT2 inhibitors warrant caution (risk of euglycemic DKA or dehydration). In a 6-week randomized crossover trial, glimepiride and liraglutide both lowered fasting glucose; glimepiride had a greater postprandial effect but more hypoglycemia.

**中文：**
HNF1A-MODY 由轉錄因子 HNF1A 的異型合子顯性變異造成；HNF1A 主要表現於肝臟、胰島 β 細胞與腎臟，點致病變異集中於 DNA 結合域。早期表現為輕度空腹高血糖與低腎閾值糖尿——由於 HNF1A 是 SGLT2 表現的關鍵因子，糖尿病發病前即出現糖尿是 HNF1A 功能不足的標誌。胰島素分泌缺陷呈進行性；若未妥善治療，併發症可與未控制的第 2 型糖尿病同樣嚴重。其他特徵包括肝細胞腺瘤、高脂血症與心血管死亡率上升。多數患者對低劑量磺醯脲類反應良好（甚至低劑量即可出現低血糖），但日後可能逐漸失效而需胰島素；meglitinide 類藥物餐後血糖較低、延遲性低血糖風險較小。SGLT2 抑制劑須謹慎（可能導致正常血糖性酮酸中毒或脫水）。一項 6 週隨機交叉試驗顯示 glimepiride 與 liraglutide 皆可降低空腹血糖；glimepiride 對餐後血糖效果較顯著，但低血糖事件較多。

## 7. HNF4A-MODY（原 MODY 1）與 HNF1B-MODY（原 MODY 5）

**EN:**
**HNF4A-MODY** shows progressive loss of insulin secretion with a phenotype similar to HNF1A-MODY (progressive β-cell dysfunction, sensitivity to low-dose sulfonylureas), but hepatocellular adenoma is not seen. Affected individuals weigh about 800 g more at birth than unaffected siblings, and hyperinsulinemic neonatal hypoglycemia that resolves spontaneously may occur; lower lipoprotein A2 and HDL cholesterol are also seen. Insulin is ultimately required as β-cell function declines; GLP1 receptor agonist benefit has been reported. **HNF1B-MODY** results from heterozygous single nucleotide variants, large deletions, or contiguous 17q12 microdeletions (the latter associated with autism spectrum disorder, ADHD, and cognitive impairment). Renal cysts and diabetes syndrome is a common presentation; other features include pancreatic hypoplasia with exocrine deficiency, reproductive tract abnormalities, abnormal liver tests, hyperuricemia, hypomagnesemia from renal wasting, and possible end-stage kidney disease. Unlike HNF1A/HNF4A, these patients do not respond to sulfonylureas; insulin is preferred, and multidisciplinary care is needed.

**中文：**
**HNF4A-MODY** 表現為隨年齡進行性胰島素分泌喪失，表型與 HNF1A-MODY 類似（進行性 β 細胞功能障礙、對低劑量磺醯脲類敏感），但不出現肝細胞腺瘤。受影響者出生體重較未受影響手足平均重約 800 g，可有出生時高胰島素血症伴自發緩解的新生兒低血糖；另可見脂蛋白 A2 與 HDL 膽固醇偏低。隨 β 細胞功能衰退終需胰島素；GLP1 受體促效劑的療效已有個案報告。**HNF1B-MODY** 由異型合子單核苷酸變異、大片段缺失或 17q12 連續基因微缺失造成（後者與自閉症類群障礙、注意力不足過動症與認知障礙相關）。腎囊腫合併糖尿病症候群（renal cysts and diabetes syndrome）為常見表現；其他特徵包括胰臟發育不全伴外分泌不足、生殖道異常、肝功能異常、高尿酸血症、腎性流失所致低血鎂，並可能進展至末期腎病。與 HNF1A/HNF4A 不同，此類患者對磺醯脲類無反應，治療以胰島素為主，且需多專科團隊照護。

## 8. Neonatal Diabetes Mellitus / 新生兒糖尿病

**EN:**
Neonatal diabetes (ND) is typically diagnosed under 6 months of age (some cases up to 1 year), characterized by insulin deficiency; infants are generally small at birth. ND can be permanent or transient. Transient ND is due to overexpression of the paternal allele at the imprinted 6q24 locus (6q24-TNDM; diabetes always transient, median remittance by 4 months, recurrence around puberty) or mildly activating variants in *ABCC8*/*KCNJ11*. Permanent ND is mostly due to K-ATP channel variants and heterozygous *INS* variants; K-ATP–related ND usually responds well to high-dose sulfonylureas. Comprehensive genetic testing — at minimum *KCNJ11*, *ABCC8*, *INS*, and 6q24 imprinting — should be undertaken in all diagnosed under 9 months, and in those diagnosed at 6–12 months if islet antibodies are negative. Rarer causes include *GATA6* (pancreatic agenesis/hypoplasia, cardiac malformations), *EIF2AK3* (Wolcott-Rallison syndrome, the most common recessive cause in consanguineous populations), and *FOXP3* (IPEX syndrome, often requiring stem cell transplant).

**中文：**
新生兒糖尿病（ND）通常在 6 個月大前確診（部分可晚至 1 歲），以胰島素缺乏為特徵，嬰兒出生時通常偏小。ND 可為永久性或暫時性。暫時性 ND 導因於印記基因座 6q24 父源等位基因過度表現（6q24-TNDM；糖尿病必為暫時性，中位緩解時間 4 個月，青春期前後復發）或 *ABCC8*/*KCNJ11* 的輕度活化性變異。永久性 ND 多由 K-ATP 通道變異與異型合子 *INS* 變異造成；K-ATP 相關 ND 通常對高劑量磺醯脲類反應良好。所有 9 個月大前確診者，以及 6–12 個月確診且胰島抗體陰性者，均應接受完整基因檢測（至少涵蓋 *KCNJ11*、*ABCC8*、*INS* 與 6q24 印記缺陷）。較罕見原因包括 *GATA6*（胰臟發育不全/未發育、心臟畸形）、*EIF2AK3*（Wolcott-Rallison 症候群，近親婚配族群中最常見的隱性病因）與 *FOXP3*（IPEX 症候群，重症常需幹細胞移植）。

## 9. Syndromic Diabetes / 症候群型糖尿病

**EN:**
Syndromic diabetes accounts for less than 1% of children in diabetes clinics. **MIDD** (maternally inherited diabetes and deafness) affects up to 1% of patients with diabetes, resulting from the mitochondrial m.3243A>G tRNA substitution (also m.8296A>G, m.14709T>C); high-energy-turnover tissues (pancreatic islets, cochlear stria vascularis) are affected. MELAS syndrome may include diabetes and the same features depending on heteroplasmy. Insulin is usually needed within 2 years of diagnosis; metformin is best avoided given mitochondrial interference and possible lactic acidosis risk. **Wolfram syndrome** (autosomal recessive, *WFS1*; prevalence ~1 in 100,000 in North America, 1 in 770,000 in the UK) presents with insulin-dependent nonautoimmune diabetes at a mean age of 6 years (range 3 weeks–16 years), followed by optic atrophy, central diabetes insipidus, and sensorineural deafness in the second decade; management is supportive. **Alström syndrome** (autosomal recessive, *ALMS1*; incidence <1:100,000 births) features cone-rod retinal dystrophy, childhood obesity, type 2 diabetes, and sensorineural hearing loss.

**中文：**
症候群型糖尿病佔糖尿病門診兒童不到 1%。**MIDD**（母系遺傳糖尿病合併耳聾）影響多達 1% 的糖尿病患者，由粒線體 tRNA 基因 m.3243A>G 置換造成（其他如 m.8296A>G、m.14709T>C）；高能量代謝組織（胰島、耳蝸血管紋）受累，故出現糖尿病與耳聾。MELAS 症候群依異質體（heteroplasmy）程度可包含糖尿病及與 MIDD 相同的特徵。多數患者確診後 2 年內即需胰島素；metformin 因干擾粒線體功能、理論上增加乳酸中毒風險而最好避免。**Wolfram 症候群**（體染色體隱性，*WFS1*；北美盛行率約十萬分之一、英國約 77 萬分之一）以胰島素依賴型非自體免疫糖尿病為首發表現，平均發病年齡 6 歲（範圍 3 週–16 歲），其後依序出現視神經萎縮、中樞性尿崩症，並在第二個十年出現感覺神經性耳聾；目前無治癒方法，以支持性治療為主。**Alström 症候群**（體染色體隱性，*ALMS1*；發生率低於十萬分之一）特徵為錐桿細胞視網膜失養症、兒童期肥胖、第 2 型糖尿病與感覺神經性聽損。

## 10. Monogenic Diabetes and Pregnancy / 單基因糖尿病與妊娠

**EN:**
**GCK-MODY:** Approximately 2% (range 0%–6%) of women diagnosed with gestational diabetes carry a heterozygous *GCK* mutation. Fetal genotype, not treatment of maternal hyperglycemia, predominantly determines birth weight: offspring of affected mothers weigh on average 450 g more than offspring of affected fathers. If the fetus does not carry the *GCK* mutation, it senses maternal glucose as high and increases insulin secretion, risking macrosomia — insulin therapy (large doses, >1 IU/kg/day per management tables) is then recommended, with consideration of delivery at 38 weeks; if both mother and fetus carry the mutation, no treatment is needed. In the US Monogenic Diabetes Registry, insulin-treated women with GCK-MODY had a 23% incidence of severe hypoglycemia, and insulin-treated GCK-affected neonates had lower birth weights. **HNF4A/HNF1A-MODY:** *HNF4A* carriers have a median 790 g higher birthweight and neonatal hypoglycemia risk; fetal growth ultrasound is recommended at 2-week intervals after 28 weeks with early delivery (elective cesarean or induction between 35 and 38 weeks). Women on sulfonylureas may either switch to insulin before conception (preferred) or continue glyburide in the first trimester and switch to insulin in the second, because glyburide crosses the placenta and increases the risk of large-for-gestational-age neonates and neonatal hypoglycemia.

**中文：**
**GCK-MODY：**約 2%（範圍 0–6%）被診斷為妊娠糖尿病的婦女帶有異型合子 *GCK* 突變。出生體重主要由胎兒基因型決定，而非母體高血糖的治療：受影響母親的子代平均比受影響父親的子代重 450 g。若胎兒未帶 *GCK* 突變，會將母體血糖視為過高而增加胰島素分泌，導致巨嬰風險——此時建議胰島素治療（依管理表建議需大劑量，>1 IU/kg/day），並考慮於 38 週引產；若母親與胎兒皆帶突變，則無需治療。美國單基因糖尿病登錄中，接受胰島素治療的 GCK-MODY 孕婦有 23% 發生需他人協助的嚴重低血糖，且帶 GCK 突變的新生兒在母親接受胰島素治療下出生體重反而較低。**HNF4A/HNF1A-MODY：***HNF4A* 突變攜帶者出生體重中位數較未受影響家人重 790 g，且有新生兒低血糖風險；建議 28 週後每 2 週以超音波監測胎兒生長，並提早生產（35–38 週間擇期剖腹產或引產）。使用磺醯脲類的孕前婦女可於受孕前改用胰島素（首選），或於第一孕期繼續使用 glyburide、第二孕期改為胰島素——因 glyburide 會通過胎盤，增加巨大兒與新生兒低血糖風險，故不建議用於第二孕期後段與第三孕期。

## 11. Conclusions / 結論

**EN:**
More than six decades after monogenic forms of diabetes were first clinically identified, the identification, molecular diagnosis, and treatment of neonatal diabetes and MODY now serve as an exemplar of diabetes precision medicine. Diabetes care providers must be aware of the clinical features, proven biomarkers, and precision therapeutics for monogenic diabetes, and continued efforts are needed to broaden understanding in currently underrepresented populations — African, Asian, South American, and Middle Eastern — to ensure equity in diabetes care.

**中文：**
自單基因糖尿病首次被臨床確認以來已逾六十年，如今新生兒糖尿病與 MODY 的辨識、分子診斷與治療已成為糖尿病精準醫療的典範。糖尿病照護者必須熟悉單基因糖尿病的臨床特徵、已驗證的生物標記與精準治療；同時仍需持續努力，將研究範疇擴及目前代表性不足的族群——非洲、亞洲、南美洲與中東人群——以確保糖尿病照護的公平性。
